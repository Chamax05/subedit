1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

Nexora Studio ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to safeguarding the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning data collection, processing, and disclosure for our mobile application Subtitle Editor and Generator (package name: com.nexora.subtitleseditor) and our official developer website.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or media files to third parties. Your media files (videos, audio, and subtitle documents) are processed locally on your device.

2. Data Collection and Media Permissions

Subtitle Editor and Generator is built as a productivity tool for video creators, translators, and editors. To provide core features, the app requests minimal device permissions:

  • Storage & Media Access (Read/Write): Required exclusively to allow you to select video/audio files from your device, generate subtitle tracks, edit timestamps, and save exported subtitle files (.SRT, .VTT, .TXT) to your storage.
  • Microphone / Audio (Optional): If you use voice-to-text recording or live transcription features within the app.

Important: Your video and audio files remain strictly on your device. We do not upload your media to private remote servers without your explicit knowledge or action.
